    How to fly

    A balloon cannot steer. It goes where the wind goes, and the wind blows a different way at every height. The ladder on the right shows each layer’s wind as an arrow; up the screen is the way you are looking. Climb or sink into the layer that heads where you want to go.

    Burner: hold to heat the envelope. It answers after a second or two, and keeps climbing a little after you let go, so think ahead. Vent: hold to spill hot air and sink faster.

    Crosses: three are laid out in the fields, each with a beam of light. Within 150 m of one, Drop marker appears. The marker keeps your drift as it falls; the dashed line shows where it would land. Closer scores more: 1000 within 3 m.

    Photos: when a landmark is in view, Photo appears. A well-framed photo scores more, and goes into your album.

    Landing: come down gently on open ground and let the basket settle. Once all three markers are down, leftover propane scores too. If you leave the valley, the flight ends.

    First light: climb above the valley rim at the start to see the sun come up before it reaches the valley floor.

    Looking around: drag the view. The camera turns back to your drift after a few seconds, and faces a cross when you are in range of it.
