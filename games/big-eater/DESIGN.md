# Big Eater

**Type:** shooter (bullet heaven). **Hook:** you are a macrophage ("big eater") on a microscope
slide. Your immune arsenal fires by itself; you steer. Germs that are weakened or tagged by
antibodies turn amber and can be *engulfed* by swimming over them: more ATP, a little health and
charge for the Burst. So the best play is to dive into the swarm you are also running from.

## Rules

- Move with WASD / arrows, or drag anywhere on touch. Space (or the Burst button) fires the
  respiratory burst when its meter is full: a wave that hurts and tags everything near.
- Germs touch you: you lose membrane (health). At 0 the culture wins.
- Kills drop ATP. Each level offers three slides: a new weapon, a new organelle, or a level of
  one you own. Five weapon slots, five organelle slots. A weapon at level 6 whose partner
  organelle you own can evolve.
- Colour is the rule: violet germs are Gram-positive (Lysozyme hits them double), pink germs are
  Gram-negative (Complement hits them double), grey specks are viruses (Interferon double),
  green are spores (tough), amber means edible or yours to collect, blue is you and your weapons,
  chartreuse is toxin: dodge it.
- A run is 7½ minutes in 25-second waves; the next wave is announced in the HUD so the build can
  be planned. Bosses at 2:30 (the Biofilm), 5:00 (the Water Bear) and 7:30 (the Superbug). A boss
  under a tenth of its health can be engulfed whole.
- Return: four cells to unlock, strains I–V (each harder), a daily culture, an atlas of germs and
  evolutions, and the lab report's culture plate.

## The moment

Engulfing a boss: time slows, the cell stretches over it, GULP, and the plate rains ATP.

## Look

Brightfield microscopy. A cool daylight-filter field with eosin-pink wisps and pale red cells;
stains carry meaning: crystal violet `#4b2a8f`, safranin `#e23d6d`, malachite green `#1f9d7a`,
methylene blue `#2b6cb0`, Lugol's iodine amber `#d99a1c`, ink `#1d1a2b`. Sprites are drawn with
multiply so overlapping germs darken like real stain. The eyepiece vignettes the corners.
Panels are glass slides with a frosted label end; upgrade choices are three slides in a tray.
Type: DynaPuff (blobby display, numbers) and Instrument Sans (text).

## Sound

Wet pops pitched by germ kind, a low "glomp" for engulfing that climbs with the chain, a
bubbling lab groove on `seq.js` that adds layers as the culture thickens.
